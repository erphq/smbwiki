# smbwiki catalog

The first ten types proved the definition format. The current checked
release contains 125 concrete business types, all published from
validated source definitions.

NAICS anchors use the official 2022 U.S. structure. Several businesses
share a code because NAICS classifies establishments by economic
activity at a broader level than smbwiki's operating-model pages.

## Completed release gates

- 25 live: close siblings tested the existing restaurant, clinic,
  and field-service bases.
- 50 live: retail, property, personal care, and professional-service
  operating models.
- 75 live: lodging, regulated care, vehicle retail, and logistics.
- 100 live: production, specialty services, and the remaining catalog
  gaps.
- 125 live: specialty construction, building systems, property
  inspection, surveying, appraisal, and interior design.

Each release requires complete business definitions, complete
new shared nodes, a zero-error graph build, rendered-page review,
internal-link validation, and public verification. Counts alone do not
complete the release.
