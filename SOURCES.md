# Source register

Primary and standards-body references used to anchor classifications,
regulated credentials, and operating details. Source links support the
structured definitions; they do not replace operator review.

## 2026-07-31 India and Singapore food-service layer

- [MoSPI, National Industrial Classification 2008](https://www.mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/main_menu/national_industrial_classification/nic_2008_17apr09.pdf):
  division 56 subclasses 56101, 56102, 56103, 56210, 56301, 56302 read
  from the official structure.
- [SingStat, Singapore Standard Industrial Classification 2025](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/-/media/files/standards_and_classifications/industrial_classification/ssic2025report.ashx):
  division 56 classes 56111, 56112, 56121, 56130, 56200 and class 10712
  read from the official structure.
- [FSSAI licensing](https://www.fssai.gov.in/cms/licensing.php): every
  food business operator registers or holds a licence under the Food
  Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration) Regulations, 2011;
  tier by annual turnover.
- [SFA, businesses that need a licence or permit for food retail](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/food-retail/licence-permit/businesses-that-need-licence-permit-for-food-retail):
  food shop licence coverage of restaurants, bakeries, coffee shops,
  snack counters, and mobile food wagons.
- [ACRA, Business Names Registration Act](https://www.acra.gov.sg/legislation/legislative-reform/business-names-registration-act/business-names-registration-act--frequently-asked-questions):
  registration with ACRA before commencing business.
- [Singapore Police Force, liquor licensing under the Liquor Control
  (Supply and Consumption) Act 2015](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/LCSCA2015):
  a liquor licence is required to supply liquor.
- CBIC GST registration thresholds (services twenty lakh rupees, lower
  in special category states) and IRAS compulsory GST registration
  above one million Singapore dollars in taxable turnover.

India state excise policy and Shops and Establishments acts are state
subjects; the bindings say so and no state-specific claim is made.

## License requirement states

Business bindings distinguish required, conditional, and commonly applicable
licenses and credentials. Each binding records the date on which its scope
statement was checked. "Required" means required for the regulated work
described by that business binding, not required everywhere in the country.
"Conditional" names the activity or jurisdiction trigger. "Commonly
applicable" identifies approvals that are widespread but not uniform across
states and localities. The operating jurisdiction's current authority remains
the source of truth.
